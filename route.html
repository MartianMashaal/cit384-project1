Play route

From good to my favorite

All four games are worth playing for different reasons. This is my current ranking, followed by the route I would give someone trying them for the first time.

My ranking

The order reflects which complete experience I would most want to revisit today.

  1. Ghostrunner

    The movement is excellent, but its demanding pace makes it a game I play in shorter bursts.

  2. Remember Me

    Some systems feel dated, yet Neo-Paris is distinctive enough that I still think about it years later.

  3. Mirror's Edge

    It is focused, visually confident, and still one of the clearest examples of movement-led level design.

  4. Cyberpunk 2077

    Night City gives me the most freedom to wander, notice details, and make a different story each time.

A weekend route

Three stops show three different approaches without asking someone to finish four full games.

Start with clarity

Play the opening chapters of Mirror's Edge and pay attention to how red objects teach the route.

Switch to precision

Try an early Ghostrunner level and notice how movement becomes faster while the route stays readable.

End by wandering

Drive across Night City without fast travel. Moving at street level reveals how different its districts feel.

Why this topic?

I have spent about 500 hours in Cyberpunk 2077, but it is not the only game that shaped how I look at virtual cities. Mirror's Edge, Remember Me, and Ghostrunner all turn architecture into part of the gameplay. I like noticing how a wall color, skyline, or narrow alley quietly tells me where to go before a map marker does.

01 / COLOR

Color as navigation

Red pipes, cyan lights, and yellow ledges can make a route readable without adding another arrow to the screen.

02 / SCALE

Vertical cities

The best cyberpunk spaces stack wealth, danger, and transportation on top of one another.

03 / MOTION

Movement as character

Running, climbing, and driving change a city from a backdrop into a place with its own rhythm.

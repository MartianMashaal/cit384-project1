The games

Four worlds, four rhythms

I chose these games because each one answers the same question differently: how should it feel to move through a future city?

2020Night City

Cyberpunk 2077

This is the world I know best. After roughly 500 hours, the appeal is less about checking icons off the map and more about recognizing places: the wet streets of Kabuki, the open desert outside the city, and the giant walls of downtown. Driving slowly makes the scale easy to notice.

10 out of 1010/10
7 out of 107/10

2008Glass

Mirror's Edge

Mirror's Edge removes most of the visual clutter found in other city games. White surfaces create breathing room while red objects point out a possible route. The city feels strict and controlled, but Faith's movement lets the player bend its rules.

9 out of 109/10
10 out of 1010/10

2013Neo-Paris

Remember Me

Remember Me stands out for the way it layers its setting. Old Parisian buildings sit underneath holograms and memory ads. That mix makes the future feel attached to a real past instead of appearing from nowhere.

9 out of 109/10
6 out of 106/10

2020Dharma Tower

Ghostrunner

Ghostrunner turns every room into a fast puzzle. The city is not a place to wander; it is a sequence of walls, rails, enemies, and gaps that must be read in a few seconds. Repeating a section until it flows perfectly is the point.

8 out of 108/10
10 out of 1010/10
